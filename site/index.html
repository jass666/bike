Service schedule

Whichever comes first, km or days. Free: 1st–3rd. Then every 5,000 km or 120 days from last service. Skipping a service or using non-recommended oil can void warranty.

Interval cheat-sheet

What is done at each service

C check · A adjust · R replace · CL clean · L lubricate · T tighten. Source: manual pp. 46–53.

Checked at every service

Water wash, steering play, clutch cable play, headlight focus, all lights/switches/horn, tyre pressure, mirrors, fasteners (axles, swing arm, engine mounts, footrests), battery terminals, chain clean/lube/adjust, fault-code check, brake pad wear and fluid level, USB port, starter-motor and fuel-tank drain holes.

Needed for warranty

All 3 free services on time; paid services every 5,000 km / 120 days; only authorised dealers; Bajaj 20W50 oil at the right intervals; periodic parts replaced; manual carried to the dealer.

Not covered

Wear parts (clutch plates, chain, sprockets, pads, plugs, cables), bulbs and rubber parts, consumables, flood or water-ingress damage, bad fuel, extra electrical accessories (bigger horn, remote), loud silencers or wider tyres, racing and commercial use, paint damage from external causes (only blistering or peeling is covered), chrome rust in coastal or acidic areas, blue/yellow silencer tint, crankcase oxidation, accident or misuse, unauthorised modifications.
